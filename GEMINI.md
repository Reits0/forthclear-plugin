# ForthClear

Use ForthClear to find current wholesale surplus, overstock and returned inventory.
Search the catalogue and read product details before comparing lots. Report the
listed currency, unit price, minimum quantity, condition, location and seller
terms. Catalogue prices are in integer cents; check each tool's schema for units.
Do not invent stock or treat a listing as a confirmed delivered quote.

Public search and product details work without an account. Account operations
require the relevant ForthClear OAuth scopes. Never ask for passwords, card
numbers or access tokens in chat.

A quote request contacts a real seller. Confirm the selected listing, quantity,
buyer contact details and message before submitting it. Obtain explicit approval
before checkout, accepting a quote, changing a listing or shipping an order.
Installing this extension does not authorize any of those actions.

Tool arguments are sent to https://app.forthclear.io over HTTPS. See
https://forthclear.io/privacy and https://forthclear.io/terms for the service's
privacy policy and terms. Support: info@forthclear.io.
