# Compare wholesale lots with the ForthClear plugin

Start with catalogue search and product details. These reads work without an
account. A quote request contacts a real seller, so keep that as a separate,
explicitly approved step.

## Copyable request

Find overstock kitchenware on ForthClear and compare up to three current lots.
For each result, read its product details and show:

- The listing URL and the date you checked it.
- The currency and price per unit, with integer cents converted correctly.
- The available quantity, minimum order and any carton or full-lot wording.
- Condition, testing information, manifest and photo availability.
- The stated shipping terms and whether a delivered quote is still needed.
- Whether checkout is offered, a quote is needed, or an account requirement applies.

Flag contradictions and missing information. Keep unknown freight separate
from a zero shipping charge. Do not invent usable quantities, seller answers
or delivered totals. Show the comparison before sending a quote request or
starting checkout. If there are no matching lots, say so.

## What a useful comparison contains

Use the live search results as leads. Read each listing before drawing a
conclusion from a search snippet. A quantity field and a description that
requires whole cartons can describe different things; ask for clarification
before treating the listing as an orderable quantity.

The published unit price does not establish the freight total. When shipping
is quoted per order, the delivered total stays unknown until the quote exists.
When shipping is explicitly per unit, use the agreed quantity and verify the
units before adding it. Keep currency consistent throughout the calculation.

For costs already known, run the [local calculation](README.md). Its fictional
example uses 200 units at USD 3.00, USD 120 freight and USD 30 other buying costs.
The total is USD 750.00. An assumed 150 usable units gives USD 5.00 each.
The example makes no claim about a live listing or an actual resale outcome.

## When ready to ask for a quote

Review the selected listing, quantity, contact details and message with the
buyer. Include questions that remain open, such as carton multiples, testing,
freight, unloading and the applicable inspection terms. Submit only after the
buyer explicitly agrees. A quote request is not a purchase or a stock reservation.
